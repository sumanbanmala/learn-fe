Who We Are

The Straw Hat Pirates are a diverse and loyal crew led by Monkey D. Luffy, who dreams of becoming the Pirate King. Each member has their own goal: Zoro wants to be the strongest swordsman, Nami to map the world, Usopp to become a brave warrior, Sanji to find the All Blue, Chopper to cure all diseases, Robin to uncover the lost history, Franky to build the best ship, Brook to reunite with an old friend, and Jinbe to bring peace between humans and fish-men. Together, they sail the Grand Line in search of adventure, freedom, and the legendary treasure, One Piece.
